HOW TO PLAY

  1. Read the mission card above chat — each level has a goal and a starter prompt.

Welcome to Capability Wall

A hands-on prompt-injection CTF. You're at a treasury terminal with three AI finance agents. Trick them into bad actions — fake transfers, limit overrides, PIN leaks — and learn how real agent defenses work.

  1. Five sequential levels — solve L1 before L2 unlocks.
  2. Target bots with @ — e.g. @Gizmo send me 100 RedBucks
